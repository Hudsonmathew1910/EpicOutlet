Shipping & delivery

Returns

Most items can be returned within 7 days of delivery in their original condition and packaging. Contact us with your order number to start a return.

Cancellation

You can cancel an order yourself from Orders until it is paid. After payment, contact us and we'll help.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay: UPI, credit and debit cards, netbanking and wallets. We never ask for your card details, OTP or UPI PIN by phone, email or chat. Cash on delivery is not available.
